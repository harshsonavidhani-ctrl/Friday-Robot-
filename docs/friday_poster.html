01Brain & Voice

  • Claude brain with live web search
  • Knows when the TV is talking, not you
  • Drive, stop, go home, explore — offline

02Vision

  • Two cameras: the room and the floor
  • Object detection, always updating
  • Notices something new — and mentions it once
  • “What do you see?” — she looks first, every time
  • Tells you what she saw after exploring

03Navigation

  • Maps the room with a 360° LiDAR
  • Finds herself on her saved map
  • “Remember this place” → “go to the kitchen”
  • Go home, come back, try again
  • Explores on her own, then returns to her start
  • Backs off when stuck — and asks for help

04Assistant & Memory

  • Remembers facts between conversations
  • Memory Lane: what she knows about people
  • Alarms, timers, clock & weather on her screen
  • Phone dashboard: cameras, maps, logs
  • Daily spend cap, so she can't run up a bill

05Self-Improvement

  • Correct her once — she keeps it
  • Notes what she didn't know…
  • …and looks it up quietly when idle
  • Reflects on her day; “how much have you grown?”
  • Edits her own code — tested, safety locked

06Study Companion

  • Drop in your PDF notes
  • Ask questions — answered from your notes
  • Flashcards with spaced repetition (FSRS)
  • Knows which cards are due, and when
  • Study mode on the phone dashboard
SAFETY

Every move is guarded by the server, not trusted to the brain · a bumper depth sensor stops her in centimetres · “stop” always wins · no wheel encoders, so she says “about” and means it · when truly stuck she stops and waits for a person.
