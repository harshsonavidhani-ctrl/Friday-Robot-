06Study Companion

HER APP

Open me on your phone and you can see what I see, drive me, set an alarm, send me exploring or study with me — and read every thought I had while doing it. No cloud in the middle: it's served straight from me.

INSTALLS LIKE AN APP

Add to home screen → her orange face is the icon, and it opens with “Welcome to Friday”. Runs over the room's Wi-Fi at https://<friday>:8443.
